# FoodCycle NC — product truth

## Product

FoodCycle NC is a local-first prototype for household food-expiry tracking, with clearly labeled beta demonstrations of neighborhood produce-sharing and pantry coordination. The primary product job is to help someone notice what should be used soon and record what they do with it.

## Audience and jobs

- A household member tracks stored food and its best-by date, location, and amount.
- A home gardener or grower can demonstrate posting surplus produce for pickup by a volunteer and delivery to a sample school, pantry, or community fridge.
- A volunteer can demonstrate claiming and completing a pickup.
- A pantry coordinator can demonstrate drafting a transfer request or client notice.
- A volunteer can keep a local demo log of hours and actions.

## Truth and constraints

- The prototype does not have real users, partner food banks, live pantry inventory, confirmed logistics, messaging, or verified impact.
- Starter listings, pantry needs, places, and baseline content are synthetic and labeled sample/demo.
- User-created state is stored only in the current browser's local storage.
- Estimated food weights are illustrative calculations; they are not measured waste reduction.
- Pantry requests and notices are local drafts only.
- Food best-by dates are not safety determinations.
