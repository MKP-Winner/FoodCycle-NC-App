# FoodCycle NC design system

## Direction

An urgency-first cooperative field ledger, staged as a readable working register rather than a generic community dashboard. Designed for a quick household inventory check in a bright kitchen and for a small local demo on an ordinary laptop or phone.

## Visual grammar

- **Mode:** Operate. Visibility of expiry, amount, storage place, and next action outranks decoration.
- **Palette:** Cool field-paper `#F3F6F4`, white work surfaces, evergreen navigation `#163A33`, pale leaf `#BFDC66`, light blue coordination callouts `#E9F2F8`, and tomato-red urgency `#A93229`. Amber distinguishes items due soon from due-now red.
- **Typography:** Segoe UI / Helvetica Neue / Arial system stack. Compact, sturdy headings and labels; tabular facts remain plain, readable sans-serif.
- **Composition:** A persistent evergreen navigation rail on desktop; a horizontal labeled navigation strip on mobile; a wide open work surface; thin register rules and aligned text instead of repeated generic cards.
- **Inventory encoding:** Colored crop-family markers, urgency-first sort, labeled expiry chips, explicit location and quantity, and direct “Used” / “Share” actions in each row.
- **Interaction:** Native form controls, inline disclosure for adding and posting, visible focus rings, status announcements, responsive row reflow, and reduced-motion support.

## Surface hierarchy

1. The food expiry tracker is the primary surface.
2. Produce sharing and pantry coordination are visibly labeled beta and explain their local-demo-only behavior before interactive lists.
3. Impact and hours separate user-created on-device demo actions from sample or externally verified outcomes.

## Content truth

All seed listings, inventory, pantry needs, and locations are synthetic sample data. The demo does not connect to partners, send notifications, verify pickups, issue certificates, or claim real impact.
