# FoodCycle NC

A responsive, local-first food expiry tracker with clearly labeled beta demo boards for neighborhood produce-sharing and pantry coordination. Sample names, listings, locations, and impact totals are illustrative only; the app is not connected to real people, pantries, inventory, messages, or delivery routes.

## Run the demo

No package manager, build step, account, or API key is required. Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The app’s JavaScript and styles load locally from the same folder.

Food entries, share-board posts, pickup claims, pantry-request drafts, and volunteer-hour logs are saved in that browser’s local storage on that device. They are not sent to a server. Use **Reset sample data** at the bottom of the page to restore the illustrative starting set.

## Try the main flows

1. In **Expiry tracker**, filter by urgency or food group, search by food or storage location, add an item, then mark it **Used** or move it to the share board.
2. In **Share board · beta**, post a produce pickup with its amount, pickup place, sample delivery destination, date, and time window. Claim an open sample pickup and mark it complete to record a local demo action.
3. In **Pantry network · beta**, draft a sample transfer request or a client notice. These actions stay local and send nothing.
4. In **Impact & hours**, log volunteer time and review your on-device demo activity. Any pound totals are estimates, not verified outcomes.

## Notes

- The expiry tracker is the primary ready-to-demo surface. The other coordination surfaces are explicitly marked beta.
- A best-by date is not a food-safety determination; use authoritative food-safety guidance when deciding whether food is safe.
- This prototype has no backend, real user accounts, live inventory, partner integrations, real-time notifications, route planning, marketplace checkout, certificates, or AI services.
